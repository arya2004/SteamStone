# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in SteamStone's issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces is expected to treat others with respect.

- Be welcoming and considerate of people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback about the work. Disagree without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not post someone's personal information, credentials, or private conversations without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexual content or unwanted advances.
- Follow maintainer requests to stop disruptive behavior.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents to [arya2004](https://github.com/arya2004) using any private contact listed on their profile. If none is available, open an issue requesting a private reporting channel without posting sensitive details.

The maintainer will review reports, respect the privacy of those involved, and respond as appropriate. Responses may include a warning, removal of content, or temporary or permanent restrictions on participation. These guidelines apply to maintainers as well as contributors.
